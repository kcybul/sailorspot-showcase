    What happens to my enquiry?

    Karol Cybul, the person behind SailorSpot, uses your email, optional name and trip preferences only to respond to your charter plan. They are stored on the SailorSpot server for up to 90 days. There is no marketing opt-in, and you do not need to send passport documents or payment details.

    For questions about your information or to request its deletion, email captain@sailorspot.ch.

    Does this page use tracking?

    We count simple anonymous visits and the campaign source, if one is present in the link. A random visit identifier lasts only for this browser session. We do not send your full page URL or referrer, use advertising cookies, or load third-party analytics. A temporary request identifier also helps prevent duplicate enquiries when you retry.

    GitHub Pages hosts this page and may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

    Can I book a yacht here?

    Not yet. This is a personal charter-planning enquiry, not a waitlist or booking form. SailorSpot is in early development and cannot confirm live prices, yacht availability or reservations. There is no obligation to proceed.

    You can explore the working app prototype; its yacht details and booking screens are demonstrations, not current offers.
